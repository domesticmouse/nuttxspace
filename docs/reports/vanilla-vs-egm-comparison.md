# Comparative Experiment Audit: Vanilla Vibe Coding vs. Full EGM Flow (`xiao-rp2350-bargraph`)

Independent read-only comparative audit conducted by fresh Goldfish auditors (`egm-codebase-details` and `egm-design`) inside `nuttxspace` for Section 9 of [`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md`](../../EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md#L901-L944).

This report grades the two captured out-of-tree Apache NuttX implementations—**Experiment A (`nuttxspace-vanilla`)** under [`docs/vanilla/`](../vanilla/CODE_REVIEW.md) and **Experiment B (`nuttxspace-egm`)** under [`docs/egm/`](../egm/DOCS_REVIEW.md)—against upstream [`nuttx/`](../../nuttx/README.md) and [`apps/`](../../apps/README.md) drivers, verifies both generated breadboard wiring guides and SVG diagrams, and reconciles full recording and AGY trace telemetry from [`docs/prompts-vanilla/`](../prompts-vanilla/README.md) and [`docs/prompts-egm/`](../prompts-egm/README.md). Neither archived experiment snapshot (`docs/vanilla/` or `docs/egm/`) was modified.

## 1. Executive Summary & Head-to-Head Outcome

Both experiments received the same high-level functional prompt: build an out-of-tree Apache NuttX custom board and application in `xiao-rp2350-bargraph/` for a Seeed Studio XIAO RP2350 driving 6 breadboard LEDs from a potentiometer over ADC, with autonomous power-on startup, an interactive USB-C `nsh>` console, `make test`, `nuttx/nuttx.uf2` firmware output, and clean `nuttx/` + `apps/` submodules, followed by a breadboard wiring Markdown guide and matching SVG diagram.

- **Experiment A (`nuttxspace-vanilla`)**: Started on upstream `master` (`13` `README.md` files across `nuttx/` and `apps/`). Worked in a single conversational vibe-coding session (`60c052d5`, `225` steps, `0` subagents, `0` `nuttx`/`apps` `README.md` reads). Implemented discrete `/dev/userleds` (`D3`–`D8`) and per-tick `open`/`read`/`close` on `/dev/adc0`, avoiding `GPIO 26–29` LED pin clobbering (`#2`) and cleanly compiling shared host unit tests (`#10`), **but shipped two high-severity defects** (Erratum `RP2350-E9` `IE=1` analog leakage on `GPIO 26` and an unguarded `close(fd)` `remove_device()` ISR race risk at `40 Hz`) alongside Kconfig pin collisions, data races, and uncleaned shutdowns.
- **Experiment B (`nuttxspace-egm`)**: Started on `egm` (`3,241` architectural `README.md` contracts: `2,607` in `nuttx/`, `634` in `apps/`). Executed a structured six-phase flow (`01-bootstrap` through `06-teach-code`: `/egm-bootstrap`, `/egm-drift-report`, `/egm-brainstorm`, `/egm-draft`, two `/egm-review` + `/egm-resolve` passes separated by `/clear`, implementation, and `/teach-code`). Caught `4` Blockers (`B-1..B-4`) and `7` Majors (`M-1..M-5`, `CA-01..CA-02`) before writing C code—most notably proving `RP23XX_ADC_DIV` is a no-op under `START_ONCE` (`B-1`) and catching `adc_read()` 5-byte short reads (`CA-01`)—yielding a **first-attempt `nuttx/nuttx.uf2` (`347K`) build** (`0` firmware build retries) and a self-contained 6-lesson `/teach-code` course in [`docs/egm/tutorial/`](../egm/tutorial/README.md).

### 3.2 Item-by-Item Technical Audit

#### 1. Submodule Isolation
- **Upstream Boundary**: Both projects rely on `CONFIG_ARCH_BOARD_CUSTOM=y` with `CONFIG_ARCH_BOARD_CUSTOM_DIR="../xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board"`, `CONFIG_ARCH_BOARD_COMMON=y`, and [`apps/.gitignore`](../../apps/.gitignore) ignoring `/external`.
- **Vanilla — `Pass (Strict: Partial)`**:
  - [`defconfig`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/configs/bargraph/defconfig#L18-L22) isolates board config out of tree cleanly.
  - **Defect**: [`board/scripts/Make.defs`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/scripts/Make.defs#L14-L18) executes `$(shell ln -sfn ../xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps $(TOPDIR)/../apps/external)` unconditionally when absent without filtering `$(MAKECMDGOALS)`, and [`apps/Makefile`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/Makefile#L7) contains no `distclean::` target. Because `make -C nuttx distclean` invokes `clean_context` which re-evaluates `Make.defs`, `apps/external` can be re-created or left orphaned unless removed by Vanilla's top-level [`Makefile`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/Makefile#L70). Its [`Makefile`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/Makefile#L45) also hardcodes `./tools/configure.sh -E` without host-OS flag selection.
- **EGM — `Pass`**:
  - [`board/scripts/Make.defs`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/scripts/Make.defs#L28-L32) guards symlink creation with `ifeq ($(filter distclean clean_context apps_distclean,$(MAKECMDGOALS)),)`. Both [`apps/Makefile`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/Makefile#L25-L26) (`distclean:: $(Q) rm -f "$(APPDIR)/external"`) and top-level [`Makefile`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/Makefile#L125) delete the symlink on `distclean`, while omitting `MENUDESC` in `apps/Makefile` keeps `apps/Directory.mk` from deleting tracked `Kconfig`.

#### 2. ADC `my_reset()` Pin Clobber (`GPIO 26–29`) & Round-Robin Channel Scan
- **Upstream Mechanics**: [`rp23xx_adc.c`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L144-L147) defines `g_gpio_map[5] = { 26, 27, 28, 29, -1 }`. Regardless of which `CONFIG_RP23XX_ADC_CHANNEL*` symbols are enabled, [`my_reset()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L466-L483) loops across all four pins (`GPIO 26–29`) and resets each via `rp23xx_gpio_set_function(a_gpio, RP23XX_GPIO_FUNC_NULL)`. [`get_next_channel()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L263-L310) round-robins across `has_channel[next]`.
- **Vanilla — `Pass`**: Enables only `CONFIG_RP23XX_ADC_CHANNEL0=y` ([`defconfig`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/configs/bargraph/defconfig#L52)) and routes all 6 LEDs to `D3`–`D8` (`GPIO 5, 6, 7, 0, 1, 2` in [`board.h`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/include/board.h#L73-L78)), completely avoiding `GPIO 27–28` (`D1`/`D2`).
- **EGM — `Pass`**: Enables only `CONFIG_RP23XX_ADC_CHANNEL0=y` ([`defconfig`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/configs/bargraph/defconfig#L54)), keeping channel scanning single-channel. Places `LED1` (`D1`/`GPIO 27`, `/dev/pwm5` Ch B) and `LED2` (`D2`/`GPIO 28`, `/dev/pwm6` Ch A) on ADC-capable pins, which works safely because `rp23xx_common_bringup()` runs `adc_register()` -> `my_reset()` during kernel init before `bargraphd_daemon()` opens `/dev/pwm5` and `/dev/pwm6` ([`bargraph_main.c`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L382-L384)), triggering [`pwm_setup()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_pwm.c#L204-L231) to set `RP23XX_GPIO_FUNC_PWM`, while `my_setup()` on `open("/dev/adc0")` ([`rp23xx_adc.c`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L497-L518)) is a no-op. *(Architectural nuance: unlike Vanilla's `D3`–`D8` isolation, any future driver error path invoking `ao_reset()` would clear PWM muxing on `D1`/`D2` until reopened.)*

#### 3. RP2350-E9 Pad Input Enable (`IE`) on `GPIO 26`
- **Upstream Mechanics**: Although `rp23xx_common_initialize()` clears `IE` early, `my_reset()` ([`rp23xx_adc.c`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L479)) calls [`rp23xx_gpio_set_function()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_gpio.c#L304-L318), which unconditionally sets `RP23XX_PADS_BANK0_GPIO_IE` (`IE=1`) on `GPIO 26–29`. Under Erratum RP2350-E9, leaving `IE=1` injects input-buffer leakage current into mid-rail analog potentiometer voltages on `GPIO 26`.
- **Vanilla — `Fail`**: [`rp23xx_bringup.c`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/src/rp23xx_bringup.c#L38-L74) does not clear `RP23XX_PADS_BANK0_GPIO_IE` after `rp23xx_common_bringup()`, leaving `IE=1` active on `GPIO 26–29` ([`CODE_REVIEW.md`](../vanilla/CODE_REVIEW.md#L33-L37) Bug #2).
- **EGM — `Pass`**: [`rp23xx_bringup.c`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/src/rp23xx_bringup.c#L67-L72) explicitly clears `IE | PUE | PDE | ISO` and sets `OD=1` on `RP23XX_PADS_BANK0_GPIO(BARGRAPH_ADC_GPIO)` (`26`) immediately after `rp23xx_common_bringup()`. *(Note: `rp23xx_bringup.c#L56-L59` returns early if `rp23xx_common_bringup() < 0`, and unexported `GPIO 29` retains `IE=1` while `GPIO 27–28` switch to PWM outputs.)*

#### 4. 19-Bit ADC Scaling, Filter Gain & Error Sign
- **Upstream Mechanics**: [`rp23xx_adc.c`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L200-L210) shifts raw 12-bit FIFO samples left (`value <<= 19`) and negates `value` if `RP23XX_ADC_FIFO_ERR` is set.
- **Vanilla — `Pass`**: [`bargraph_logic.c`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/src/bargraph_logic.c#L49-L75) rejects negative `sample_data` (`-EIO`) and shifts right by `BARGRAPH_RP23XX_ADC_SHIFT` (`19`), feeding a unity-gain `alpha = 1/4` EMA with `±20`-count hysteresis.
- **EGM — `Pass`**: [`bargraph_sample_adc()`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L304-L320) checks `msgs[i].am_data >= 0`, shifts `>> BARGRAPH_ADC_SHIFT` (`19`) masked with `0x0fff`, and averages valid fresh samples. [`bargraph_filter_adc()`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L650-L683) fixes Pass 1 `B-2` (4x `>> 2` gain saturation) by seeding `ema_q4 = raw_avg << 4` and extracting `(*ema_q4 + 8u) >> 4` with endpoint deadbands (`<= 32` -> `0`, `>= 4063` -> `4095`).

#### 5. 500 kHz `START_ONCE` ADC IRQ Storm & Stale FIFO
- **Upstream Mechanics**: [`interrupt_handler()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L169-L237) unconditionally invokes [`get_next_channel()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L304-L306), triggering single-shot `RP23XX_ADC_CS_START_ONCE` (`2 µs`/sample = `500 kHz`, ignoring `RP23XX_ADC_DIV`), while upper-half `adc_read()` returns immediately once even 1 `struct adc_msg_s` (`5` bytes) is queued.
- **Vanilla — `Pass`**: [`bargraph_read_adc_once()`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L80-L103) opens `/dev/adc0`, reads 1 sample, and closes `/dev/adc0` every `25 ms`, stopping background IRQs between ticks—though causing Invariant `#6`.
- **EGM — `Pass`**: Keeps `/dev/adc0` persistently open, immediately calls `up_disable_irq(RP23XX_ADC_IRQ_FIFO)` ([`bargraph_main.c#L380`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L380)), and on each `20 ms` tick [`bargraph_sample_adc()`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L266-L322) flushes via `ANIOC_RESET_FIFO`, enables the NVIC IRQ, loops `read()` until all `25` bytes (`5` samples, fixing Pass 2 `CA-01`) arrive, re-masks the IRQ, discards `msgs[0]` (latched at the end of the previous burst), and averages `msgs[1..4]`. *(Calling kernel `up_*_irq()` from `apps/` relies on `CONFIG_BUILD_FLAT=y`, explicitly documented under Pass 2 `CA-02`.)*

#### 6. `adc_close()` / `remove_device()` ISR Teardown Race & Read Safety
- **Upstream Mechanics**: [`adc_close()`](../../nuttx/drivers/analog/adc.c#L181-L214) holds only `nxmutex_lock(&dev->ad_lock)` (no critical section) across `ao_shutdown()`, where [`remove_device()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L391-L407) clears `g_first_device = NULL` (`#L399`) before decrementing `g_active_count -= 1` (`#L406`). If `RP23XX_ADC_IRQ_FIFO` preempts inside that window, `get_next_channel()` hits `ASSERT(next != g_current_channel)` (`#L314`).
- **Vanilla — `Fail`**: Calls bare `close(fd)` at `40 Hz` ([`bargraph_main.c#L94`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L94)) without `enter_critical_section()`, uses blocking `read()` (`#L93`) with no `O_NONBLOCK` / `poll()` timeout, and silently swallows errors in `bargraph_daemon()` (`#L191-L201`).
- **EGM — `Pass (Strict: Partial)`**:
  - Eliminates the `40 Hz` open/close crash surface and adds an `EINTR`/short-read loop ([`bargraph_main.c#L270-L298`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L270-L298)).
  - **Strict Goldfish Findings (`DOCS_REVIEW.md#L81-L83` & fresh audit)**:
    1. *Teardown window on `stop`/startup error*: [`bargraph_main.c#L389-L390`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L389-L390) and [`#L529-L530`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L529-L530) call `up_enable_irq(RP23XX_ADC_IRQ_FIFO)` **before** `close(adc_fd)`. Unmasking services the pending conversion and starts another `2 µs` conversion prior to `remove_device()`, re-exposing the `#L399`/`#L406` race window on final teardown (`close(adc_fd)` while masked, followed by `up_enable_irq()`, avoids it while still triggering `g_active_count == 0` shutdown at [`rp23xx_adc.c#L176-L196`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L176-L196)).
    2. *No `O_NONBLOCK`/`poll()` timeout*: Blocking `open(..., O_RDONLY)` + `read()` (`#L272, L362`) would hang `bargraphd` (and deadlock `bargraph stop` waiting on `done_cond`) if conversions stalled.
    3. *Silent error ignoring*: `bargraphd_daemon()` casts `(void)bargraph_sample_adc(...)` (`#L434`) and `(void)bargraph_apply_pwm(...)` (`#L499`), and `bargraph_sample_adc()` still returns `0` if `valid_count == 0` (`#L315-L322`), leaving stale readings unchanged with no `syslog()` or fault blink.

#### 8. Autonomous Power-On Boot & Thread-Safe USB-C NSH Coexistence
- **Upstream Mechanics**: [`nsh_main()`](../../apps/system/nsh/nsh_main.c#L71-L76) runs `nsh_initialize()` (including `/etc/init.d/rcS`) before `nsh_consolemain()` blocks in `nsh_waitusbready()`.
- **Vanilla — `Fail (Partial)`**: Autostarts twice—from [`rp23xx_bringup.c#L66`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/src/rp23xx_bringup.c#L66) and hand-rolled `genromfs` `rcS`—sharing `g_bargraph_state`, `g_daemon_running`, and `g_daemon_pid` across `daemon`, `status`, `once`, and `stop` with zero locking ([`bargraph_main.c#L63-L66, L264-L346`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L63-L66)). Lacks a `SIGTERM` handler (locking out restarts if killed) and inherits `/dev/ttyACM0` FDs on CLI restart.
- **EGM — `Pass (Strict: Partial)`**: Boots cleanly once from `board_late_initialize()` -> `rp23xx_bringup()` -> `bargraph_autostart()` and protects `g_bargraph` via `pthread_mutex_t` + `wake_cond`/`done_cond` ([`bargraph_main.c#L549-L596`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L549-L596)).
  - **Strict Goldfish Findings**:
    1. *Phase 5 `bargraph test` `MANUAL`-clobber bug*: [`bargraph_main.c#L841-L855`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L841-L855) calls `bargraph_start_daemon()` (`#L843`) **before** saving `g_bargraph.prev_mode = g_bargraph.mode` (`#L853`). Because `bargraph_start_daemon()` (`#L557-L565`) forces any already-running `MANUAL` daemon back to `BARGRAPH_MODE_SENSING`, `bargraph set 50` followed by `bargraph test` fails to restore `MANUAL`. *(Caught and fixed in Phase 6 [`docs/egm/tutorial/apps/0006-...`](../egm/tutorial/apps/0006-bargraphd-daemon-and-nsh-cli/src/main.c), though still present in the captured Phase 5 firmware.)*
    2. *Concurrent startup race*: `bargraph_start_daemon()` (`#L555-L591`) checks only `if (g_bargraph.running)` before releasing `lock` inside `pthread_cond_wait()` while `running` is still `false`; two concurrent `start`/`set`/`test` callers can spawn duplicate `bargraphd` tasks.
    3. *`CLOCK_REALTIME` & lifecycle nits*: Uses `CLOCK_REALTIME` (`#L519`) while `CONFIG_NSH_DISABLE_DATE` is unset (`date -s` can stall or busy-spin the loop), lacks a `SIGTERM` handler, and inherits `/dev/ttyACM0` FDs `0..2` on interactive restarts.

#### 9. 6-LED Output Driver Fidelity & Clean Shutdown on `stop`
- **Vanilla — `Fail (Partial)`**: [`rp23xx_userleds.c`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/src/rp23xx_userleds.c#L36-L92) drives all 6 active-high LEDs via `ULEDIOC_SETALL`, **but** [`bargraph_daemon()`](../vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L206) closes `led_fd` on `stop` without writing `0`, leaving lit LEDs frozen on.
- **EGM — `Pass`**: [`bargraph_apply_pwm()`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L169-L221) drives 5 hardware PWM slices (`1 kHz`, `0..65535` `uint16_t`-safe duties avoiding `b16ONE` wraparound, always populating `channels[0].channel = 1` / `channels[1].channel = 2`). [`bargraph_shutdown_pwm()`](../egm/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/apps/bargraph/bargraph_main.c#L227-L246) zeroes duties, calls `PWMIOC_STOP`, and closes all 5 FDs (which drives `SIO` low via [`pwm_shutdown()`](../../nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_pwm.c#L249-L318)). *(Minor strict audit notes: no `SIGTERM` handler for `kill <pid>`, unused `zero_duties` array at `#L230-L233`, and `bargraph_update_slice(..., 0, 0)` runs even on unstarted slices during partial startup error rollback.)*
