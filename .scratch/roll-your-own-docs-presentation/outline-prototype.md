## Pacing & Density Architecture (`30:00` / `1,800s` Total)

- **Total stage steps**: **`214` steps (`S001`–`S214`)** grouped under **`26` narrative beats (`Beat 01`–`Beat 26`)** across **5 acts**.
- **Visual transition budget**: **`208` low-density visual steps** across `1,618.4s` (`26m 58.4s`), averaging **`7.8s` per transition** (every non-cast step strictly **`7.0s–9.0s`**, inside the `5–10s` target).
- **Live terminal budget**: **`6` `[Live-cast]` playback beats** across `4` standalone players totalling **`181.6s` (`3m 02s`)**, plus **`3` freeze-frame players** (`0s` playback).
- **Transition taxonomy**:
  - `[Slide]` (`96` steps): A fresh, uncluttered slide carrying a single headline, quote, stat, diagram, or short code excerpt.
  - `[v-click]` (`88` steps): A progressive reveal adding one bullet, badge, table row, or callout to the current slide.
  - `[Code-step]` (`24` steps): A Shiki line-highlight step advancing `2–4` lines at a time through code, Kconfig, or report diffs.
  - `[Live-cast]` (`6` steps): Full-viewport warped `asciinema` playback via `[` / `]` / `p` + chapter pills (`102` total Slidev deck slides = `96 [Slide]` + `6 [Live-cast]`).

#### Beat 05 (`04:15–05:15`, `60.0s`, `8` steps) — Supporting Traps #1 & #2: Silent Kconfig Drops & `my_reset()` + Erratum `RP2350-E9`
- **`S029` (`04:15–04:23`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Supporting Trap #1 (Build Config)** — Trace Step 180 (`docs/vanilla/CODE_REVIEW.md`): Vanilla enables `CONFIG_ADC=y` in `defconfig`. *Takeaway:* First casualty: NuttX's Kconfig dependency tree.
- **`S030` (`04:23–04:30`, `7.0s`) `[v-click]`**: **Silent `olddefconfig` Drop** — Missing `CONFIG_ANALOG=y` silently strips `CONFIG_ADC=y` (while `CONFIG_DEV_GPIO=y` collides on `GPIO 5,6,7,0,1,2`). *Takeaway:* Grep doesn't warn you when `olddefconfig` silently discards a peripheral driver.
- **`S031` (`04:30–04:38`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Supporting Trap #2 (Silicon Init)** — `my_reset()` inside `nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c`. *Takeaway:* Next casualty: an invisible side effect inside kernel bringup.
- **`S032` (`04:38–04:45`, `7.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `rp23xx_adc.c#L466-L483` (`g_gpio_map[5]`) — Highlights loop iterating across **all four pins (`GPIO 26–29`)**, even when only Channel 0 is enabled. *Takeaway:* Even if you only touch `A0`, `my_reset()` resets `GPIO 26` through `29`.
- **`S033` (`04:45–04:53`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `rp23xx_adc.c#L479` -> `rp23xx_gpio_set_function()` — Highlights unconditionally setting `RP23XX_PADS_BANK0_GPIO_IE` (`IE=1`). *Takeaway:* Calling `set_function()` quietly switches the digital input buffer back on.
- **`S034` (`04:53–05:00`, `7.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Silicon Erratum `RP2350-E9`** — Leaving `IE=1` injects input-buffer leakage current into mid-rail analogue potentiometer readings on `GPIO 26`. *Takeaway:* On RP2350 silicon, leaving `IE=1` on an analogue pin corrupts mid-rail ADC measurements.
- **`S035` (`05:00–05:08`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `docs/vanilla/xiao-rp2350-bargraph/board/src/rp23xx_bringup.c#L38-L74` — Highlights missing pad isolation after `rp23xx_common_bringup()`. *Takeaway:* Vanilla never clears `IE | PUE | PDE | ISO` or sets `OD=1`, shipping `RP2350-E9` leakage straight to the board.
- **`S036` (`05:08–05:15`, `7.0s`) `[v-click]`**: **Trap Summary** — Grep gives you symbol names, never cross-layer silicon errata invariants. *Takeaway:* And those two were only the warm-up acts before our primary technical trap.

#### Beat 06 (`05:15–07:00`, `105.0s`, `13` steps) — Primary Spine (Part 1): The 500 kHz `START_ONCE` Storm & Vanilla's 40 Hz Teardown Race
- **`S037` (`05:15–05:23`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Primary Spine Setup** — Meet the `500 kHz` `START_ONCE` ADC storm inside `rp23xx_adc.c`. *Takeaway:* This single driver quirk threads right through both experiments.
- **`S038` (`05:23–05:31`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `nuttx/arch/arm/src/rp23xx/rp23xx_adc.c#L169-L237` — Highlights `interrupt_handler()` unconditionally calling `get_next_channel()`. *Takeaway:* Every time an ADC sample finishes, the interrupt handler asks for the next channel.
- **`S039` (`05:31–05:39`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `rp23xx_adc.c#L304-L306` — Highlights back-to-back `RP23XX_ADC_CS_START_ONCE` re-arming inside the ISR. *Takeaway:* Instead of pausing, the driver fires another single-shot conversion immediately.
- **`S040` (`05:39–05:47`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **The Storm Metric** — **`500 kHz` (`2 µs`/sample) interrupt flood** whenever `/dev/adc0` stays open! *Takeaway:* Half a million interrupts a second if you naively leave `/dev/adc0` open.
- **`S041` (`05:47–05:55`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Vanilla's Workaround** — `bargraph_read_adc_once()` in `docs/vanilla/.../bargraph_main.c#L80-L103`. *Takeaway:* Vanilla noticed the driver kept running, so it improvised a workaround in user space.
- **`S042` (`05:55–06:03`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `bargraph_main.c#L80-L94` — Highlights `open("/dev/adc0")` -> blocking `read()` -> `close(fd)` at **`40 Hz` (`25 ms` ticks)**. *Takeaway:* Forty times a second, Vanilla opens the ADC, reads one sample, and slams it shut.
- **`S043` (`06:03–06:11`, `8.0s`) `[v-click]`**: **Why That Looked Clever** — Closing `/dev/adc0` stops conversions between ticks, dodging the `500 kHz` storm. *Takeaway:* On the bench, CPU usage drops and the LEDs work—looks like a smart hack, right?
- **`S044` (`06:11–06:19`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **Zoom Into Upper-Half Teardown** — `nuttx/drivers/analog/adc.c#L181-L214` (`adc_close()`). *Takeaway:* Look inside NuttX's upper-half `adc_close()` path to see what happens on `close(fd)`.
- **`S045` (`06:19–06:27`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `adc.c#L181-L214` — Highlights `nxmutex_lock(&dev->ad_lock)` with **zero `enter_critical_section()`** around `ao_shutdown()`. *Takeaway:* Holding a mutex only blocks other threads—it does nothing to mask hardware interrupts.
- **`S046` (`06:27–06:35`, `8.0s`) `[Code-step]`**: `rp23xx_adc.c#L391-L407` (`remove_device()`) — Highlights `g_first_device = NULL` (`#L399`) *before* `g_active_count -= 1` (`#L406`). *Takeaway:* Inside `remove_device()`, the device pointer goes null seven lines before the active count drops.
- **`S047` (`06:35–06:43`, `8.0s`) `[Slide]`**: **The Teardown Race Diagram** — `RP23XX_ADC_IRQ_FIFO` fires between `#L399` and `#L406` -> `get_next_channel()` hits **`ASSERT(next != g_current_channel)` (`#L314`)**! *Takeaway:* If the 2-microsecond interrupt lands in that tiny window, NuttX hits an assertion panic.
- **`S048` (`06:43–06:51`, `8.0s`) `[v-click]`**: **Risk Multiplier** — Calling `close(fd)` at **`40 Hz`** rolls those race dice forty times every second! *Takeaway:* Vanilla turned a rare teardown bug into a `40 Hz` reliability timebomb.
- **`S049` (`06:51–07:00`, `9.0s`) `[v-click]`**: **Read Safety Nits** — Blocking `read()` with zero `O_NONBLOCK` / `poll()` timeout and silently swallowed errors in `bargraph_daemon()`. *Takeaway:* One stalled conversion hangs the daemon loop silently forever.
